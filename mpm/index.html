The performance of Welte-Mignon roll 225, Alfred Grünfeld playing Schumann's Träumerei, reconstructed as Music Performance Markup with MPM Desk. The four files are the ones the editor saves; the viewer reads them from here.

transcription.mei
The score as MEI, with one <recording> per take of the roll.
work.json
The reconstruction: the transformer calls, and the claims about the performance they are grouped under.
performance.mpm
The MPM those calls wrote.
score.msm
The score, as MSM, that the MPM is performed against.

Every segment of the reconstruction, one claim about a stretch of the performance, has the identifier https://welte225.org/mpm/<id>, which opens the viewer at that segment.
