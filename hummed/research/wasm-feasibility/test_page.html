VocalTractLab in the browser

VocalTractLab 2.4, compiled to WebAssembly, synthesizes 10 seconds of the Träumerei hum here, on your computer. The test checks whether this page may run it, how fast it runs, and whether two voices fit at once. It takes about a minute, and the fan may spin up.
