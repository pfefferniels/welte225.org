Welte-Mignon roll 225

WM 225

Alfred Grünfeld plays Robert Schumann's Träumerei, recorded in Vienna on 20 January 1905.

Three copies, two at Stanford and one in the collection of Marc Widuch Four versions, A, A1, B and C

This roll is the case on which the dissertation Grünfelds Geist by Niels Pfeffer demonstrates its methods. Everything the projects below produce about it is published here, so that the roll, its copies, its versions and every single perforation can be referred to by a stable address.

Projects

Roll Desk
The edition of the roll: its copies collated, its versions and their edits, the editorial assumptions behind them, and an emulation of the Welte-Mignon that plays any version. opensourcelinked-rolls
MPM Desk
Reconstructs the recorded performance as Music Performance Markup: the recording set note against note with the score, the differences fitted as performance instructions and grouped into claims. opensource
Welte Ads
A TEI collection of Welte's advertisements, the company's own account of what the rolls were meant to be. opensource

Identifiers

Every entity of the edition has an IRI under this domain, and each of them opens the edition at that entity in Roll Desk: the edition itself is https://wm225.org/, a copy is https://wm225.org/copy/<id>, a symbol read from the roll is https://wm225.org/symbol_<id>, and a hole on a copy is https://wm225.org/<id>. The identifiers are stable; the data behind them will grow.
