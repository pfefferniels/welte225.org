This site collects the various projects that are taking shape as part of my dissertation Grünfelds Geist. All of them have Grünfeld's recording of Schumann's Träumerei for Welte-Mignon (1905) as their subject.

Projects

Roll Desk
The edition of the roll, its copies collated, its versions and their edits, the editorial assumptions behind them. opensourcelinked-rolls
MPM Desk
Reconstructs the recorded performance as Music Performance Markup: the recording set note against note with the score, the differences fitted as performance instructions and grouped into claims. opensource
Welte Ads
A TEI collection of Welte's advertisements, the company's own account of what the rolls were meant to be. opensource

Data

Roll edition
The edition of WM 225 as JSON-LD in the Roll Edition Format, licensed CC BY 4.0. edition.jsonldformat
Vocabulary
The Roll Edition Ontology, a small extension of CIDOC CRM and LRMoo for editions of piano rolls, with its type vocabulary and the T-100 expression types. ontologytypes

Identifiers

Every entity of the edition has an IRI under this domain, and each of them opens the edition at that entity in Roll Desk: the edition itself is https://welte225.org/, a copy is https://welte225.org/copy/<id>, a symbol read from the roll is https://welte225.org/symbol_<id>, and a hole on a copy is https://welte225.org/<id>. The identifiers are stable; the data behind them will grow.
