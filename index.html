Träumerei, hummed

Alfred Grünfeld's 1905 Welte-Mignon roll of Schumann's Träumerei, played from its reconstruction, while a pianist's inner voice hums along: a bass line written out in MEI, sung as you listen by VocalTractLab, here in your browser.

Exaggeration takes the performance from plain (0) to twice Grünfeld's expression (2); the voice always goes a step further than the piano. Drag the spectrogram to move through the piece.
